<h1 class="hi">Hi, I'm Haotong Sun!</h1>

Welcome to my personal web page! I am a Researcher at [**Infindeep AI**](https://infindeep.com/) in Hangzhou, working on AI for biology. Previously, I led LLM research at [**WiseDiag AI**](https://wisediag.com/) and worked on AI for drug discovery at [**MindRank AI**](https://www.mindrank.ai/). I received my M.S. in Computer Science and Technology from Nanjing Normal University.

My research lies at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and Biology.

- **AI:** large language models, LLM agents, and recursive self-improvement.
- **Biology:** protein & peptide design, generative modeling of protein dynamics, and molecular world models.
